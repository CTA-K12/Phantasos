# Help

Phantasos stores, processes and serves media for CTA applications. The applications
talk to its API; these pages are for the people who manage it.

- **Dashboard** — activity at a glance.
- **Clients & Keys** — the applications allowed to use the API, and their keys.
- **Processing** — uploads that failed or got stuck, with retry.
- **Users** — who can sign in here.
- **Audit Log** — who did what, including every file served.

Click **?** on any page for help specific to it.
